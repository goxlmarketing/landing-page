Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 August 2026

This policy explains how GoXL Ally, a GoXL initiative (“GoXL Ally”, “we”, “us”), collects and uses information when you visit our website or register for early access.

1. Information we collect

  • Your name, email address and phone number when you register for early access.
  • Your Terms acceptance, email-marketing preference, consent time and policy version.
  • Basic technical and security information that may appear in website or hosting logs, such as IP address, browser and device information.

2. How we use your information

  • To receive, manage and respond to your early-access registration.
  • To send registration confirmations, access invitations and essential product or service updates.
  • To send product news, founder insights and promotional emails only when you choose the optional email-marketing checkbox.
  • To protect the website, prevent misuse and comply with applicable law.

Your phone number is collected for early-access contact and will not be used for promotional SMS or WhatsApp messages under this consent.

3. Your choices and consent

Registering for early access requires us to process the details needed to manage your request. Promotional email consent is optional and is not required to register.

You may withdraw promotional email consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link in an email or contacting us at hello@goxl.ai.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising.

Service providers that are necessary to operate GoXL Ally—such as website hosting, secure data storage and email-delivery providers—may process limited information only to provide those services on our instructions. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Retention

We keep registration information only for as long as reasonably necessary to manage early access, provide requested communications and meet legal or security requirements. Marketing preferences are retained while you remain subscribed. When information is no longer required, we delete or anonymise it where reasonably possible.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal information. No internet or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, update or delete your personal information, withdraw consent, or raise a privacy concern. Email hello@goxl.ai and include enough information for us to identify and respond to your request.

8. Children

GoXL Ally is intended for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the early-access form.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as GoXL Ally develops. The effective date above will show the latest version. For questions or complaints about this policy, contact hello@goxl.ai.
